    Selected projects

    ShefGuide: AI support for international students

    MSc dissertation, 2026. React, TypeScript, FastAPI, MongoDB, GPT-4o-mini, Gemini

    A web app that answers international students’ academic and settling-in questions using retrieval-augmented generation, with two language models behind one interface.

    • Every request passes a security layer first: a rate limiter, a prompt-injection filter and PII redaction.
    • I compared the two models on 30 questions. They were statistically indistinguishable on academic support (4.73 vs 4.67), and free-tier rate limits turned out to be the bigger practical risk.

    Chat-based personalised health assistant

    BSc final-year project, Babcock University, 2025. Group of three. Python, Streamlit, MongoDB, GPT-4 API

    A health assistant for people managing their own wellbeing, built around the shortage of accessible healthcare support in Nigeria.

    • Sign-up captures a health profile, and a dashboard summarises it with BMI and basic metrics.
    • GPT-4 generates lifestyle and workout plans, answers health questions in a chat consultation, and suggests dates for doctor visits.
    • Conversations, plans and appointments are stored per user in MongoDB, and passwords are stored hashed.
    • Built in agile iterations and tested at unit, integration and system level.
